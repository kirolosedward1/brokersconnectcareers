## HSTS preload, before submitting

`preload` in the header does nothing until the domain is submitted at hstspreload.org, and that cannot be undone quickly. Every subdomain then has to serve HTTPS: `www`, `careers`, `careers2`, and anything the mail provider asks you to put in DNS (MX and TXT records are unaffected; an `http://` tracking subdomain would break). The apex `brokersconnect.net` must also send the header on its redirect. Submit only once all of that holds.
