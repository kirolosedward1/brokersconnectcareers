## The app on the phone

- **Session**: AES-256-GCM, the key in the Keychain (this device only, after
  first unlock); `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` false is right for this.
- **What stays on the phone**: only the taxonomy is persisted across launches;
  the last signed-in role (never an authority); hidden companies and
  consultants (ids only, device-local by design); the welcome's answer.
  Signing out clears personal caches, the picked files and the export
  (`src/lib/personal-cache.ts`). "Download my data" writes to the cache and
  deletes it after the share sheet closes.
- **Logging**: two `console.warn` calls, neither with personal data (an
  unmapped auth error's code and message; i18n errors in development only).
  No crash reporting or analytics SDK.
- **Permissions, iOS**: photo library and camera (camera only for company
  papers), each asked when used, purpose strings in Arabic and English;
  notifications asked from a card on Home, never at launch; Face ID declared
  (see "Decide" below).
- **Permissions, Android**: no `READ_MEDIA_*` (the system photo picker is
  used), storage permissions capped at Android 12, `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`
  blocked, `RECORD_AUDIO` blocked. Full list in `docs/app-store.md`.
- **Third parties the app talks to**: Supabase (data, auth, storage), the
  website on Vercel, Expo (updates and push relay), Apple and Google (sign-in
  and push delivery), Cloudflare Turnstile (only when Supabase asks for a
  captcha). All are in the privacy policy's list of processors.

## For the owner — legal or business decisions

These cannot be settled in code. Each says who should decide.

1. **"Shared with third parties" on Play's Data safety form** (legal). The
   recommendation is "No", since an application sent to a company and a
   directory card the consultant chose to show are transfers the person
   starts. A lawyer may read the directory differently.
2. **Egypt PDPL (Law 151/2020)** (legal), carried from September: transfers
   abroad (Supabase in Mumbai, Vercel, Resend, Expo, Cloudflare) and whether
   a licence or local representative is needed; the legal basis for each
   processing purpose; the retention periods below.
3. **Retention periods still open** (business, then legal) — `docs/data-lifecycle.md`
   "Decisions": `email_suppressions` after deletion, `audit_events`, abandoned
   sign-ups, notifications (180/365 days), reports/appeals/support requests,
   names in `admin_audit_log`, and the production backup schema
   `backup_2026_09_29`, which still holds every account deleted since
   29 September — drop it once nothing needs it.
4. **Face ID purpose string for an app lock that does not exist yet**
   (business). `expo-local-authentication` ships ahead of the feature so it
   can arrive over the air. Apple asks that purpose strings describe actual
   use; nothing ever shows the prompt, but a reviewer reading the Info.plist
   may ask. Either ship the lock before submitting, or drop the module and its
   string from the first build and add them with the lock (a new build).
5. **The company-owner deletion promise** (business): "a reply within 7 days,
   deletion within 30" (`account.deleteRequested`) must be a commitment the
   team can keep.
6. **Billing** (business, then legal): if `BILLING_ENABLED` is turned on,
   Apple's label and Play's form gain purchase history, and `docs/legal.md`
   "Before billing opens" applies.
7. **Analytics** (business): if visit counting is turned on, `docs/legal.md`
   "Before visit counting is switched on" first, then both stores' answers
   change (usage data).
