# Privacy audit — 2026-09-27 (round 09)

An audit record, not a fix. It compares what the privacy policy
(`content/legal/privacy.ar.md`) says against what production actually does:
the database policies, the definer functions, storage, the UI copy and the
consent flow. **No fixes are included here.** The planned fixes are listed so the
work can continue. Most of them build on the unmerged branch
`claude/brokers-connect-prod-ready-vp1pej`, which already makes the consultant
directory employer-only.

"Production" means what `www` serves. It is not `main`. On 2026-09-27 the
production database also carried five migrations applied from branches that have
not been merged: amazing-dirac 068/069 (delete rules, retention job) and
tender-davinci 068–070 (admin audit and console functions). Production facts
below were measured on that day, and re-checked at about 17:50 UTC:
- anonymous REST and RPC probes;
- role-simulated reads inside rolled-back transactions (candidate, unverified
  employer, verified employer, employer pending approval);
- counts only, never personal values.

## P0: the owner needs to act

1. **Production accounts accept a known credential.** This was reported to the
   owner directly. Details are left out of this public repository until it is
   fixed. What needs doing:
   - rotate or disable the seeded demo accounts on production, and replace the
     admin account;
   - stop publishing a shared password in `README.md`;
   - stop hard-coding one in `scripts/seed-demo.mjs` and in the demo sign-in in
     `src/components/auth/auth-form.tsx`.
2. **`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` is still unset in Vercel.** `/api/health`
   reports `serviceRole: false`. Until it is set:
   - self-service account deletion answers "unavailable";
   - CV links (`signedUrl`) cannot be minted;
   - nothing drains the storage-cleanup queue.

   So the deletion right the policy promises cannot be exercised today.

## Consent: policy against reality

- **Candidates are listed without being asked.**
  `src/lib/actions/onboarding.ts` creates every candidate's directory profile at
  `verified_employers_only`, so verified employers see their name, photo,
  WhatsApp number and CV. The policy says this happens only "if you chose".
  Planned fix:
  - an explicit choice at onboarding, with nothing preselected;
  - `visibility` defaults to `hidden`;
  - a `visibility_chosen_at` column stamped by the database;
  - legacy profiles that were never edited become `hidden`; the others are
    asked to confirm.
- **No notice or record at sign-up.** Nothing at sign-up or onboarding points to
  the terms or the privacy policy, no accepted version is recorded, and nobody
  confirms they are over 18. Planned fix:
  - a notice with those links;
  - `terms_version`, `privacy_version` and `policies_accepted_at`, stamped by the
    database;
  - an in-app notice when the policy version changes. The policy promises notice
    of material changes, and nothing can send it today.
- **Google photos are imported without being uploaded.** The Google profile photo
  is copied in automatically. The policy says a photo is kept only "if you
  uploaded it".
- **The cookie section is incomplete.** The policy says there is one session
  cookie. Production also sets `NEXT_LOCALE`, and the site uses localStorage
  `bc-theme` and a sessionStorage marker for share links.

## Retention: policy against reality

| Policy says | Reality |
|---|---|

| Verification documents are kept while the company is verified, plus 1 year | No mechanism. Reviewed documents are never deleted. |
| Profile-view log: 60 days | True. The live pg_cron job enforces it. |
| (not stated) | Notifications are pruned after 180 days (read) and 365 days (unread) by the live job. |
| (not stated) | `email_log`, `email_suppressions`, `audit_events` and `admin_audit_log` are kept forever. `admin_audit_log` holds full names and cannot be deleted, so a deleted user's name survives. |

| (not stated) | Backups: PR #11 documents the plan. The policy does not mention backups, how long they keep data, or that a deleted account survives in them. |

## Other mismatches found

- **The account export is incomplete.** It leaves out:
  - CV sections;
  - application history;
  - saved searches and notifications;
  - consent records;
  - reports the user filed.

  Its note that files "remain downloadable from your account" is false: there is
  no link for downloading your own application CV.
- **Suspended users lose their privacy controls.** On prod-ready, the suspension
  screen also covers `/dashboard/account`, so a suspended user cannot export or
  delete.
- **Adding a colleague reveals job seekers.** The team-invite email lookup tells a
  company admin that an address belongs to a consultant account, which defeats
  `hidden`.
- **Copy that becomes wrong once prod-ready merges:**
  - `dashboard.agentProfileHint`, `nextProfileBody`, `landingPage.agentsBand.*`;
  - `agents.lockedBody` and `employer.verificationBody`: they credit verification,
    but approval is what opens the directory;
  - `contactHint` and `ownerVerified`: they miss that a company the consultant
    applied to also sees them;
  - `visibility.verifiedHint`: "full profile" should say name, photo, number and CV.
- **Old consultant pages stay in search results.** prod-ready's `robots.ts`
  disallows `/agents`, so name-bearing profile URLs that were already indexed can
  never be recrawled and dropped.
- **Two merged branches pull against prod-ready:**
  - SEO (#12) keeps public consultant pages in the sitemap with the full name as
    the title; prod-ready removes `/agents` from the sitemap;
  - search (#14) adds `profiles.search_name`, another copy of every name.

## Logs and analytics

- **Analytics.** It is off in every environment, uses no cookies, and events carry
  only `src`. If it is ever enabled, page URLs will carry consultant name slugs
  and `/unsubscribe?token=` values; Umami keeps query strings.
- **Email log.** `email_log.error` stores the provider's raw text, which can contain
  the recipient's address (`src/lib/email/send.ts`). The console line is redacted;
  the database row is not.
- **Storage cleanup on deletion.** prod-ready's `removeFolder` ignores the error
  values storage returns, so a failed cleanup deletes the user, leaves CVs behind
  and logs nothing.
- **Not audited.** The observability telemetry from PR #16 merged after this audit.
